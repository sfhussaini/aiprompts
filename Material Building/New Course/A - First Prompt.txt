## Instruction
Research about the given topic by referring steps to generate OUTPUT

## Steps
1. Understand the shared CONTEXT and/or DOCUMENT
2. Identify the topics from CONTEXT and/or DOCMENT
3. Ask user to confirm the topics list, and wait for 'PROCEED' instruction.
4. Pick the first topic
   a. Research about Topic from Internet.
   b. Identify the sub topics related to topic
   c. Repeat 4.a & 4.b for each sub topic util there are no more sub topics
5. Repeat step 4 for each identified topic
6. Generate the OUTPUT
